---
title: On This Day in the Activity widget
tags: [end user, site admin]
---

The Dashboard's **Activity** widget has a new **Published On This Day** section that lists posts published on today's date in earlier years, so you can find older posts to revisit or share again. Recent comments move out of Activity into their own **Recent Comments** widget ([#65116](https://core.trac.wordpress.org/ticket/65116)).
