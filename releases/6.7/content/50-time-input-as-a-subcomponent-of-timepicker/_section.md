Sometimes users only need a way to enter time separate from the [date for the TimePicker.component](https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/pull/63145). Developers can now offer `separateTimePicker.TimeInput` fields in their plugins and apps. 

**Adoption approach:** [Documentation is available](https://wordpress.github.io/gutenberg/?path=/docs/components-timepicker--docs) via Storybook.
**Key Make Posts/GitHub/Trac Issue(s):** TimeInput: Expose as subcomponent of TimePicker ([63145](https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/pull/63145)). 

Developer Note excerpt: [TimePicker: Time input can be used in isolation](https://make.wordpress.org/core/2024/10/18/editor-components-updates-in-wordpress-6-7/#timepicker-time-input-can-be-used-in-isolation)
