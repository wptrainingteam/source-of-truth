A new screen option lets you turn off moving meta boxes, so they stay where they are on edit screens ([r64301](https://core.trac.wordpress.org/changeset/64301), [#50699](https://core.trac.wordpress.org/ticket/50699)). Focus states are also easier to see in high contrast mode ([r64823](https://core.trac.wordpress.org/changeset/64823), [#45910](https://core.trac.wordpress.org/ticket/45910)).
