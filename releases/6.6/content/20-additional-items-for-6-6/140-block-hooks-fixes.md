Efforts in this cycle focused on technical improvements and fixes, including a [fix](https://github.com/WordPress/wordpress-develop/pull/6358) that shows the toggle for hooked blocks that were added by a filter to an \_un\_modified template and a [fix](https://github.com/WordPress/wordpress-develop/pull/6604) for the [harmonization for the ignoredHookedBlocks metadata injection logic](https://core.trac.wordpress.org/ticket/60759).

**Adoption approach:** Learn more about using [block hooks in this developer blog post.](https://developer.wordpress.org/news/2024/03/25/exploring-the-block-hooks-api-in-wordpress-6-5/)
