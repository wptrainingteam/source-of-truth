After its debut in 6.5, this release cycle focused on bug fixes and smaller enhancements rather than any new functionality. This includes the following:

*   Update loading visual with new progress bar component ([54797](https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/issues/54797))
*   Font Library: show the scrollbar bottom edge ([54401](https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/issues/54401))
*   Clarify which fonts are uploaded vs theme provided in modal ([55220](https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/issues/55220))
*   Fix and improve handling of uploading font files ([60835](https://core.trac.wordpress.org/ticket/60835)).
