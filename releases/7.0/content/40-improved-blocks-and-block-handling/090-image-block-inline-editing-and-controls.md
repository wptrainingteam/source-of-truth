WordPress 7.0 comes with a revamp of the image editing feature in the editor. It’s now easier to crop, rotate or zoom in on a particular image corner. ([#72414](https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/pull/72414)) ([#73277](https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/pull/73277)).

#### Advanced Image Controls [site admin][end user]

Image block now supports the focal point control and aspect ratio adjustments for wide and full alignments, plus reorganized inspector controls with a dedicated content tab. [#73115](https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/pull/73115), [#74519](https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/pull/74519), [#74201](https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/pull/74201)
