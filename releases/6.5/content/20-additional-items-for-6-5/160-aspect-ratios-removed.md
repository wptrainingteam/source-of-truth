For aspect ratio, all instances have been [removed for the 10:16 and 16:10 aspect ratios](https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/pull/55053). While this is not anticipated  to impact most themes, it’s recommended to check if you were using these aspect ratios in any patterns or templates for images.

**Key Make Posts/GitHub/Trac Issue(s):** Remove 16:10 from 10:16 options from AspectRatioDropdown ([55053](https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/pull/55053))
