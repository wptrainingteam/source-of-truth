Now designers and creators can choose between a <div> or <hr> tag, opening up more styling possibilities for this block. The setting to switch can be found under **Advanced > HTML Element**. The transformation option now also includes the Spacer block. ([67530](https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/pull/67530)) ([66230](https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/pull/66230))
