The outcome of the Image manipulation methods are now better communicated in the block editor. The success notices are displayed at the bottom of the editor. The notices also come with a handy **Undo** link to revert to the original if necessary ([67314](https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/pull/67314), [67312](https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/pull/67312)).
