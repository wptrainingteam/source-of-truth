Reset colors for blocks and global styles in the editor with a single click thanks to the inline reset button added to all color controls ([#67116](https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/pull/67116)). The Shadow panel and the Duotone settings also received a very handy reset button. Instead of the need to remove settings one at a time, designers can quickly start over.
