In WordPress 6.2, the distraction-free mode was introduced to the _Post_ Editor. Building on this experience, this tool has now been added to the _Site_ Editor, offering a more frictionless site-wide editing experience as well as delivering feature parity with other Editors. As with the Distraction Free Mode in the Post Editor, this works by hiding various parts of the editor interface. When enabled, any open sidebars are closed, and toolbars fade away, leaving your content to take center stage. This offers a nearly 1:1 preview of your site, a long requested piece of feedback. You can toggle this mode on/off as you’d like.
