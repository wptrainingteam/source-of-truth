[After first being suggested in 2017](https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/issues/1890) as a block, Footnotes are available, allowing you to add comments, citations, etc., to your content with ease at the bottom of your post or page. This works for both pages and posts. 

**Visuals:** [video demo](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Dp8cqsfsZ0TUHubTswWGfMZOexDfcbQZ/view?usp=sharing)

**Key Make Posts/GitHub/Trac Issue(s):** Footnotes: try with post meta ([51201](https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/pull/51201)), Footnotes: register meta field for pages ([52024](https://github.com/WordPress/gutenberg/pull/52024))
